# Credits - JOURNEY MODE content

**Story, dialogue and deck design:** Atos Vinicius (concept, protagonist, original chapters 1-5 in Portuguese, the CLT cards and the "Atos Vinicius" duelist) with Grok Bot (English translation of chapters 1-5, new chapters 6-8, deck lists, the Cockatiel character).

**Cockatiel:** an original fictional duelist created for this mode. The name only refers to the bird; no real person's name, face or voice is used.

**Characters and world:** Yu-Gi-Oh! and all its characters, cards and the Forbidden Memories game are the property of Kazuki Takahashi / Konami. This is an unofficial, non-commercial fan project; it needs the player's own legal copy of the USA disc (SLUS-01411) and ships none of its data.

**Code base:** Yu-Gi-Oh-Forbidden-Memories-Recompiled (MIT) by Unchiga and contributors, built on krystalgamer/memories-decomp.
