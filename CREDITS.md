# Credits

JOURNEY MODE is an unofficial, non-commercial fan mod for Yu-Gi-Oh! Forbidden Memories. It ships no game data; you need your own legal copy of the USA disc (SLUS-01411).

## Author
* Atos Vinicius: concept, story, protagonist, CLT cards, "Atos Vinicius" duelist, direction.

## Built on
* **Yu-Gi-Oh! Forbidden Memories Recompiled** (native PC port, MIT) by Unchiga and contributors: https://github.com/Unchiga/Yu-Gi-Oh-Forbidden-Memories-Recompiled . Mod loader, renderer and engine this mod runs on. Contributors include MaChInEgUn3, Unchiga, Cody, Yamyi, d02d02, DaVinciLord and Tiago S S Gaspar.
* **krystalgamer/memories-decomp** (decompilation of the game), the base of the port: https://github.com/krystalgamer/memories-decomp . Not redistributed here.

## Third-party assets and code
* Fantasy Town Kit 2.0 by **Kenney** (CC0): https://kenney.nl

* PSX Nature assets by **heyheythere** (CC BY 4.0): credit "PSX Nature by heyheythere, CC BY 4.0"

Written with help from Grok Bot (English translation, chapters 6-8, deck lists, the Cockatiel character). Yu-Gi-Oh! and all related characters and cards belong to Kazuki Takahashi / Konami.
